## Introduction

Yunbei Urban Construction is a city‑building mod focused on the realistic recreation of **urban traffic infrastructure**. It assembles road blocks, road signs, municipal facilities, traffic devices and practical utilities, all modeled after real‑world counterparts.

**Blocks & Items:**

- **Road Blocks**: A complete standardized road block system covering base roads, white‑yellow dual‑color markings, single‑double lines, thick markings, bevel markings, right‑angle markings, T‑junction markings, arrow markings, cross‑road markings, diamond markings and more. All textures strictly follow the current national traffic standard **GB 5768.3‑2025** to guarantee compliance.
- **Road Markings**: Ground markings including straight‑ahead, turn, U‑turn, lane merge, yield‑to‑slow, stop‑and‑yield, crosswalk advance warning, vehicle‑distance confirmation, minimum/maximum speed limit, dedicated lanes (bus, non‑motor vehicle, electric bicycle, high‑occupancy‑vehicle etc.) and disabled‑access markings. Made in accordance with GB 5768.3‑2025 for versatile road‑scene construction.
- **Traffic Signs**: A reproduced traffic‑sign library with 366 models in total. It covers cross‑road warnings, sharp bends, steep slopes, narrow bridges, tunnels, pedestrian/child/non‑motor‑vehicle warnings, no‑entry, speed/height/width/mass restrictions, stop‑to‑yield, oncoming‑traffic yield, one‑way roads, roundabout guidance, lane directions, parking‑space signs and many others, referencing GB 5768.2‑2022.
- **Municipal Facilities**: Road lamps, pole assemblies, text display screens, customizable flags, detection cameras, radar speed detectors, supplementary lamps, gantry‑mount systems (with LED panels), height‑limit bars, warning nets, anti‑glare nets / anti‑glare boards, multi‑color crash barriers (including slanted / double‑bay / chamfered variants), reflective markers, traffic islands (with edge strips and inner fillings), instrument pole assemblies, highway guardrails (iron / green), road flower boxes, trash cans, construction barriers, warning bollards, crowd‑control barriers, crash‑proof drums, traffic cones, speed bumps, rumble strips, wheel‑stops and other models for urban‑road and highway accessories.
- **Traffic Devices**: Interconnected traffic‑light systems (straight‑ahead / left‑turn / pedestrian signals), controllable via wand and link‑wand linkage. Gate‑barrier systems (main gate, stepped base, horizontal / vertical bars), height‑limit bars, crowd‑control barriers, warning bollards, guardrails and other traffic‑management & safety‑protection devices for traffic‑control scenarios.
- **Practical Utilities**: Versatile custom‑function wands: General Wand, Tree Wand, Link Wand, Water Source Wand, Rotation Wand, Text‑Copy Wand, to boost scene‑building efficiency and interactivity.
